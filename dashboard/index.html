Method, in full

  1. Pre-token means what the record says. A protocol is on the board when DefiLlama records no token for it: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. That is a record, not a promise — a protocol on the list may still hold a token its record does not carry.
  2. Peer multiple. For each category, the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of its live protocols, shrunk toward the global median with a prior of 8 peers, clamped to 0.02–1.20. Categories with no live member use the global median.
  3. Growth. The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50…+100%, moves the value by 0.35× the change: a protocol up 100% in a week is worth 1.35× its base, one down 50% is worth 0.83×.
  4. Maturity. Age runs 0.70 for a newborn to 1.00 at three years; a protocol with no listing date takes the snapshot's median age.
  5. What this is not. The board does not predict whether or when a token is issued. Implied value is what the token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. Farm plans are generic steps from the protocol's category, chains, size and age — never a guarantee of an airdrop.
